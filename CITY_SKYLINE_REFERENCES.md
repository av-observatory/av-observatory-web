# City Card Skyline References

The city card backdrops in `src/components/CityBackdrop.tsx` are original, simplified vector illustrations of recognizable skyline features. These Wikimedia Commons photographs were used as visual references; their pixels are not served or copied into the site. The images' individual licenses and credits remain on their linked pages. For non-city geography (freight corridors, statewide routes), the card uses a road graphic instead of a fabricated skyline.
