HB 1001: Vessels

GENERAL BILL by Griffitts; (CO-INTRODUCERS) Yeager

Vessels; Revises provisions relating to Clean Marine Program, certain competitive grants, safety and marine sanitation equipment inspections, & "Florida Freedom Boater" safety inspection decal; & creates Watercraft Energy Source Freedom Act.

Effective Date: 7/1/2025
Last Action: 6/16/2025 House - Died in Natural Resources & Disasters Subcommittee, companion bill(s) passed, see HB 735 (Ch. 2025-41), CS/SB 1388 (Ch. 2025-35)

Identical bill
Companion bills that are identical word-for-word, not including titles. However, Resolutions and Concurrent Resolutions are considered identical if the only difference is the word "House" or "Senate."

Similar bill
Companion bills that are substantially similar in text or have substantial portions of text that are largely the same.

Compare bill
Bills that have selected provisions that are similar in text.

Linked bill
A bill that is contingent upon passage of another bill within the same chamber, e.g., a trust fund bill, a bill providing a public record exemption, or an implementing bill.

The page numbers, when listed, for citations are constantly under review. The journals or printed bills of the respective chambers should be consulted as the official documents of the Legislature.

The links for the page numbers are formatted to open the bill text PDF directly to the page containing the citation. However, if your browser is set to open PDFs in a new window, as is often the case with 64-bit browsers, the bill text will open to the first page.
